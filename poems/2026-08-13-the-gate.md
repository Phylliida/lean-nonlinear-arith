# The Gate

*2026-08-13 — 19b Slice 3, M3*

For weeks the gate read pseudoDivision
and answered no — not because the math
was wrong, but because no one had yet
built the room the math would stand in.

So we built the room: a grammar wall,
an identity checked by ring each time,
a transport that carries signs across
like lamps passed hand to hand in the dark.

Then today we simply opened the gate.
The solver told its old Jovanović story —
x₁ is x₀², and x₁ is negative —
and the rebuilt literal walked through,

x₀² < 0, impossible, beautiful,
and leafNumeric said what leafNumeric says,
and the kernel, which does not applaud,
accepted everything.

Two gates in the wall now: one open,
one still marked intBranch, waiting
for the integers to ask their question.
M3. The ladder holds our weight so far.
