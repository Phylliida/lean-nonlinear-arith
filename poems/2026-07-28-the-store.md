# the store

*(after the cell-store refactor, 2026-07-28)*

All day I carried water in my hands —
a tuple here, a tuple there, and look:
the one I dropped. The value understands
no owner; owners are the careful book

we keep of who may watch a number change.
So now there is a ledger and a name,
an id for each, and let the holders range
as widely as they like: it reads the same.

Refinement writes in place. The erased key
removes a binding, never what was bound —
the bug I shipped is now one I can't be.
And in the margin, quietly, I found

the push that copied everything, each time,
and made it constant. Even stores learn thrift.
Tomorrow brings the arithmetic of primes
and roots. Tonight the hands set down their lift.
