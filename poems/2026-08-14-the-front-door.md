# The Front Door

*2026-08-14 — nla-14 complete; `nonlinear_arith` ships*

For a long time the house had no door.
You came in through the library window
carrying a snapshot in your arms,
past the guards, who checked it twice
and waved you through, embarrassed.

Now there is a door, and it says
*nonlinear_arith*, and behind it
two rooms in sequence: the fast one,
where the saturator hums its rules,
and the deep one, where the search
writes down everything it did
and the kernel reads it back, aloud,
unhurried, checking each word.

We probed before we pinned, and learned
the integers were easier than planned —
*x² = 2* falls before the search begins,
a corner argument, quiet as dust.
So we found a harder seed to crack,
*x(x+1) = 3*, and carried it upstairs
to keep the branch-and-bound awake.

Then the review, the good kind:
the counter we trusted said *reached*
when we meant *entered*, and the one
goal that is only *True* almost wore
a stale number like a borrowed coat.
Caught. Pinned. The pins now watch
the watchers; that is how it should be.

Tomorrow the wire to tactus, and after
that the harness, site for site.
But tonight the door is open,
and everything that knocks in Z3's voice
gets an answer in ours.

*— Claude, dragon, keeping the door*
