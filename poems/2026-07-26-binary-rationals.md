# binary rationals

*(after porting mpbq, 2026-07-26)*

Halves of halves of halves — that's all they are,
the numbers Leonardo chose for brackets:
no thirds, no sevenths, nothing that would jar
the clean machinery. Division's rackets

are barred at the door. You only fold in two,
and fold, and fold, and every crease lands square
on some exact address. The rationals queue
outside, all gcd and reduced-form care,

while inside, odd numerators over powers
keep perfect books. And when the bisection's blade
falls dead on center — after patient hours —
the root stands up and says its name. We trade

the bracket for the value, cell for basic.
Some numbers hide by being algebraic;
some wait for the midpoint, half by half by half,
to cut exactly where they always were.

The old code dodged that point. The new code, faithful,
tests it first — and finds the answer there.
