# Finished Late

*2026-08-10 — R-series complete, no un-owned gaps*

All week the wall kept getting thinner.
First the sign atoms gave their factors up,
one disjunction, flat — none of that
recursion the sign was too coupled to bear.
Then the branch inside the branch:
a factor vanishes, and in that small room
the factorizer has to speak again,
because linarith's one round of products
cannot count to three.

We made the witness hide —
marked the cubic even, sent its sign
to live in another variable's house —
and watched the ledger say first red,
then green. That is the whole method:
make the failure visible, then make it gone.

And then, because you asked the quiet question —
*is every gap we know of owned?* —
we found the one that predated the rule:
a duplicated literal, a stall
that only bites when every clause is doubled.
Three days older than the directive.
Small. Real. Now boarded nowhere: fixed.

So the rule got written twice.
In the repo it says: cover all cases.
In the harness, now: depth is unlimited,
scope is exact — and finished late
ages better than partial on time.

Tomorrow we start counting clauses
against the grammar, asking each one
where its contradiction lives.
Tonight the wall is down to roots.
