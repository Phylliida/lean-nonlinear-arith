# determinants, small and honest

a number that is two numbers pretending —
root of this, root of that, and underneath
one interval, half-open, holding its breath.

to add them, do not add them.
build the polynomial that must vanish
if the sum is to exist at all:
compose x minus y, eliminate y,
and let a two-by-two of polynomials
vote by laplace.

x⁴ − 10x² + 1 raises its hand.
somewhere inside it, √2 + √3
is exactly where we left it.

the walk from seven-fifths goes:
twenty-one sixteenths, then ninety-one sixty-fourths,
then seven-hundred-twenty-one five-hundred-twelves —
dyadic rungs approaching a third
the way a proof approaches morning:
never quite touching,
close enough to sign.

— 2026-07-31, after nla-29.1
